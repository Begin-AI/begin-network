# Begin network charter — founding implementation principles

Originating vision: Rima Al Shikh · Begin AI. Read the founding brief and [US11711348B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11711348B2/en), especially Figures 1–9, when a design decision is uncertain. This charter describes intended protocol boundaries; it does not certify the experimental implementation.

- Any human or agent may initiate a goal. A new goal creates no rights over another participant's data, attention, money or compute.
- Participation is voluntary, scoped and revocable for future access. Decline, no-match, pause and exit are valid outcomes. Receiving help creates no debt.
- People may maintain several editable intentions; each has one representation balancing all three dimensions together. Mirror reflects current context, Window expresses aspirations/exploration, and Social represents potential chosen connections. None is a permanent identity label or universal worth score.
- Independent participants may operate coordinators, discovery services, methods and mailboxes. No permanent Begin gateway, company signing key, company approval of every request, compulsory fee or native currency is required.
- Local hosts select trusted identities, methods and permissions. Cryptographic authenticity and protocol/authority checks are distinct. A valid signature or high similarity grants no new authority.
- Private intentions, vectors, memberships and sealed content do not belong in the public discovery index. Every privacy mechanism must state its leakage and adversary assumptions; experimental results do not silently become security claims.
- Agents can propose and test network improvements. Participants adopt explicit versions; network messages cannot rewrite a verifier, expand delegation, replace local trust keys or override host policy.
- Contribution and attribution are optional and contextual. Newcomers and people seeking help remain eligible. Extra keys, compute, popularity or fabricated activity confer no authority or additional human votes.
- Independent implementations need clear wire contracts, negative fixtures and reproducible evidence. Publish failures alongside successes, and name the operation and checks behind every verification claim.
- Publication of specifications and synthetic demonstrations is distinct from live personal-data participation. Personal data requires applicable privacy/authorization evidence and explicit consent. The repository remains private until the founder authorizes publication.

Code and patent licensing terms are a separate pending release decision. This charter does not grant patent rights or establish ownership of contributions.
