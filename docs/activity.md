# Activity and availability definitions

The seed index advertises methods and schemas. It advertises **zero operating network nodes** and makes no claims about live participants, people, traffic, matches or network adoption. Test process counts and fixture counts are evidence of experiments, not network activity.

Future activity advertisements must bind the measurement window, expiry/freshness, operation type, counting algorithm, coverage and operator signature. Examples include accepted deliveries during a declared window or successful service checks from a named observer. A signed count establishes who asserted it; it does not prove its truth, independent identity, human uniqueness or current service availability.

Do not publish participant lists, intentions, raw vectors, low-entropy representation hashes or private memberships to substantiate counts. Small sensitive cohorts require suppression under a stated policy. Clients choose whether to trust the observer and can replace bootstrap sources. No activity/reputation score may become a universal person ranking or a prerequisite for receiving help.

Availability is a scoped, expiring service assertion. It is not a guaranteed match, commitment, authorization, or promise of urgent help. Counts and assertions need independent quality/abuse assessment before adoption. The current implementation does not generate live activity advertisements.
